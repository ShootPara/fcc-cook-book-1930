# Site guide

## Audience and design

The site is designed for people who may be older, unfamiliar with complex websites, or using a keyboard, screen reader, touch screen, or printed copy.

The interface deliberately favors:

- a short, consistent header;
- a visible search field followed immediately by the chapter catalog;
- high-contrast navy, white, and blue controls;
- large labeled controls with keyboard focus indicators;
- an open-by-default, collapsible chapter catalog modeled on a directory rather than a modern “discovery feed”; and
- a large-print recipe view with a dedicated print action.

There is no account, sign-in, or hidden navigation requirement.

## Finding recipes

### Simple search

Enter a title word or ingredient, such as `apple`, `soup`, or `cake`. The site searches titles, descriptions, ingredients, and directions.

### Excluding an ingredient

Prefix a term with `-` to leave it out. For example:

```text
apple -sugar
```

finds recipes that mention apple but do not mention sugar in the searchable recipe text.

### Browse by chapter

Choose a chapter in the cookbook catalog to filter the recipes. Select **All chapters** to return to the complete collection.

The chapter catalog appears immediately below search and is displayed when the page opens. Select its heading to collapse it when you want more room for results. Choosing a chapter applies that chapter as a filter and returns the visitor to the top of the recipe list.

All 31 chapters appear in the catalog. Results start with 24 recipes; select **Show more recipes** to reveal the next 24. The count shows how many are visible out of the total, and changing a search or filter starts a fresh set.

Recipe cards show the chapter, title, a short description, and available contributor and page details. The recipe detail screen goes directly from the title to ingredients and directions.

## Recipe pages

Every recipe page provides:

- an ingredients list separate from directions;
- adjustable text size;
- a print action with the cookbook title and compiler at the top, plus a source citation and full HathiTrust URL; and
- the Version 8 source ID, PDF page, printed page, and contributor where available.

The site currently publishes the English Version 8 text. Portuguese recipe translations are a planned derived edition, not an on-page machine-translation feature.
